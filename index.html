The official home of Grandma's original recipe

INGREDIENTS
  • 3oz Philadelphia Cream Cheese
  • 1/2 cup Heinz Ketchup
  • 1/2 tsp dry mustard
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 tbsp onion salt or minced onion (then add more to taste)

Allow cream cheese to soften. Add cream cheese, ketchup, mustard and sour cream together in bowl. Add onion to taste. With a Ninja or other high powered blender/food processor, blend until dip has creamy consistency. Chill and serve.

Grandma recommends Lay's Classic potato chips.

MOM'S ALTERNATIVE VERSION

Substitute onion with half a packet of Lipton Onion Soup mix. Add extra ketchup to taste.

Mom recommends Utz potato chips.

FOR A LOWFAT VERSION

Try substituting sour cream with plain greek yogurt!
